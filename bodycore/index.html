BodyCore

Body tracking, training and nutrition in one iPhone app.

Left and right tracked separately

Log measurements and lifts per limb to spot imbalances that most apps average away.

Training and nutrition together

Detailed workout logging with sets, reps, weight and failed sets, plus strength charts per exercise, alongside food tracking.

Free barcode scanning

Scan foods and build recipes with gram or per-serving amounts, powered by Open Food Facts.

Private by design

Your data stays on your iPhone. No accounts, no ads, no tracking.
