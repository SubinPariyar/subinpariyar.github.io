BodyCore Privacy Policy

Effective 9 November 2026

BodyCore is an iPhone app for tracking your body, workouts and nutrition. It is built by Subin Pariyar, an independent developer in the UK. In short: your data stays on your iPhone, and I don't collect any of it.

What stays on your iPhone

Everything you enter, including your profile (such as name and date of birth), body measurements, weight, workouts and food logs, is stored only on your device. It is protected by iOS data protection and is not sent to me or to any server I run. BodyCore has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

Food lookups (Open Food Facts)

When you scan a barcode or search for a food, BodyCore sends that barcode number or search text to Open Food Facts, a free public food database, to look up the product. Nothing else from the app is included. Open Food Facts handles these requests under its own privacy policy.

Camera

The camera is used only to read barcodes while you are scanning. No images are saved or sent anywhere.

Backups

You can export backups to the Files app or iCloud Drive. These are created only when you choose to, or by the automatic backup setting if you turn it on, and they go only where you save them. Backup files are not encrypted, so keep them somewhere private.

Feedback and crash reports

Notes you write in BodyCore's feedback section stay on your phone until you copy or share them yourself. If you're testing through TestFlight, Apple may share feedback, screenshots and crash reports you choose to send. These are covered by Apple's privacy policy.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes all its data from your iPhone. Any backups you exported remain where you saved them until you delete them.

Children

BodyCore is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through TestFlight feedback while BodyCore is in beta.
